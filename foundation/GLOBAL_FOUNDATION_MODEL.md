# Global Foundation Model

## Purpose
A research architecture for connecting real assets, productive capacity, financial systems, digital assets, liabilities, and cash flows without treating market capitalization as equivalent to real wealth.

## Core principle
Financial claims must remain distinct from underlying real assets, productive capacity, cash flow, and liabilities. The system must not assume that cryptocurrency or equity market capitalization can directly repay sovereign debt.

## Layers
1. Real assets: land, housing, infrastructure, machinery, energy, minerals, agriculture, water.
2. Productive capacity: factories, logistics, services, technology, human capital.
3. Financial system: banks, bonds, equities, insurance, pensions, credit.
4. Digital assets: crypto assets, tokenized assets, RWA markets, subject to source and license restrictions.
5. Liabilities: sovereign, corporate, household, banking-sector and external liabilities.
6. Cash flows: tax revenue, wages, business revenue, trade, investment income and debt service.
7. Circular economy: waste, recovery, recycling, secondary materials and reuse.

## Global Balance Sheet Engine
Track separately:
- Stock: assets and liabilities at a point in time.
- Flow: GDP, income, production, trade and cash flow over a period.
- Claims: securities, loans and other financial claims.
- Capacity: potential productive output.
- Resource base: physical and ecological resources.

Never add overlapping financial claims together as if they were additional net wealth.

## Digital Market Layer
CoinMarketCap or other licensed market-data sources may be used as an observation layer for crypto-asset prices, market capitalization, volume, supply, exchange markets, historical data and RWA categories. Market capitalization is not treated as sovereign wealth or as direct debt-repayment capacity.

## Debt Transformation Engine
Research scenarios that improve debt sustainability through:
- higher productive output;
- lower resource and energy costs;
- stronger domestic supply chains;
- improved trade balance;
- infrastructure productivity;
- recycling and resource efficiency;
- sustainable fiscal cash flow;
- transparent restructuring where appropriate.

The engine must not create money, erase legal obligations, or represent hypothetical assets as settled debt.

## Data lineage
Every observation should retain:
`source`, `source_type`, `license_status`, `reference_period`, `published_at`, `retrieved_at`, `methodology_version`, `verification_status`.

## Foundation objective
Create an open, auditable research framework for understanding how resources, production, finance, digital assets and liabilities interact across countries and over time.
