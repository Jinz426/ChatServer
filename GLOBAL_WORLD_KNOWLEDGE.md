# Global World Knowledge

> A research framework for understanding the physical world through coordinates, objects, evidence, resources, supply chains, prices, history, and AI analysis.

## Vision

From one observation point, progressively connect:

`coordinate → space → buildings → people → roads → commerce → products → prices → quality → production → supply chain → raw materials → land/energy/water → lifecycle → recycling → history`

The objective is not to control the world or automatically access external networks. The objective is to create an open, evidence-based framework that authorized people, organizations, and AI systems can use to observe, compare, verify, and understand the world.

## Observation layers

1. **Coordinate** — latitude, longitude, time, geographic context.
2. **Spatial design** — residential areas, shops, markets, factories, farms, infrastructure, protected land.
3. **Mobility** — pedestrian, bicycle, motorcycle, car, truck, bus, rail, port and air connections.
4. **Objects and products** — visual identification, category, brand, model, size, material.
5. **Economics** — local price, unit price, wages, rent, energy, transport, tax, exchange rate.
6. **Supply chain** — manufacturer, supplier, raw material, logistics, destination.
7. **Quality and lifecycle** — durability, repairability, maintenance, reuse, recycling and residual value.
8. **Evidence** — source, timestamp, provenance, confidence, competing explanations.
9. **History** — changes in land use, population, industry, infrastructure and prices over time.
10. **Simulation** — clearly labeled scenarios and forecasts; never presented as established facts.

## Evidence principle

Every important claim should distinguish:

- **Observed** — directly visible or measured.
- **Sourced** — supported by an external source.
- **Derived** — calculated from known data.
- **Inferred** — a reasoned interpretation.
- **Unknown** — not currently established.

Confidence must never be used as a substitute for evidence.

## Forensic-inspired methodology

The system uses forensic-style reasoning for provenance and verification:

`observation → evidence → hypothesis → independent corroboration → alternative explanations → conclusion → revision`

Correlation must not automatically be treated as causation.

## Global history model

History is represented as a connected system rather than only a chronology:

`time × space × people × resources × technology × institutions × economy × environment`

New evidence should be able to revise previous interpretations while preserving version history.

## Product value model

A product's observed retail price is not assumed to equal its intrinsic value or production cost. Analysis should separate:

`materials + energy + labor + capital + land + manufacturing + packaging + logistics + storage + tax + retail + risk + margin`

Actual costs should be sourced where possible; estimates must be explicitly labeled.

## Safety and governance

- No unauthorized network access, scanning, surveillance, or device control.
- No automatic collection of private or sensitive personal information.
- Use public, licensed, consented, or otherwise lawfully obtained data.
- Preserve provenance and timestamps.
- Minimize personal data.
- Make uncertainty and limitations visible.
- Allow corrections and versioned revisions.

## Long-term direction

The long-term research goal is a machine-readable global knowledge graph that can help answer questions such as:

- Why was a city or industrial district designed this way?
- Why do prices differ between regions?
- Where do physical products and materials originate?
- How do land, resources, infrastructure and people interact?
- Why do some places grow while others decline or become vacant?
- What resource and recycling loops can be improved?
- How can different AI systems independently analyze and cross-check the same observation?

## Guiding idea

> See the object. Understand its relationships. Trace the evidence. Reconstruct the system. Then return to the object with a deeper understanding.

This is the project's starting specification, not a claim that a complete global model already exists.
